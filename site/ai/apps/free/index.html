How this ranking works

This page ranks consumer AI apps and chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and more) by how useful they are on their free tier — a blend of public usage signals collected daily and a small set of hand-maintained factors reviewed by the maintainer. Automated signals are collected once per day from public APIs and snapshotted into data/free-apps_snapshots/; the hand-maintained factors live in config/free_apps.json and only change when a human updates them.

Automated data sources (collected daily)

GitHubstars and 30-day star deltas — only for apps with an open-source repo. All other apps show –.
Hacker Newscomment mentions for the trailing 7 and 30 days (Algolia API).
Redditmentions for the trailing 7 and 30 days (OAuth search, newest-first, capped at 1000).

Hand-maintained factors (maintainer review)

The Free tier, Ease of access and Usefulness columns are human ratings (0–100) with an accompanying note, edited in config/free_apps.json and updated by pull request as products change their free tiers. They are hand-marked in the table, always present for every app (no coverage penalty), and are subjective — but they capture exactly the thing the other signals can't: how much of an app you get without paying.

Scoring

Each metric is min-max normalized across the tracked apps to 0–100. Drag the weight sliders to re-weight — the ranking recomputes instantly in your browser.

  • Free Tier Value — how much a free user gets: hand-reviewed free-tier, ease-of-access and usefulness ratings plus 30-day HN/Reddit mention volume and stars.
  • Momentum — recent buzz: 7-day and 30-day HN/Reddit mentions and 30-day star deltas.

Coverage penalty

Missing sources count as zero in the composite. Apps with no open-source repo (most of these) have no star count, so they lose the weight that metric carries — visible via the n/m sources badge. The hand-maintained factors never trigger this penalty.

Pricing tags

The Free / Freemium / Paid tag next to each app is hand-maintained from each product's official pricing page — an approximation, not a metric, and it does not affect the score.

Caveats

  • Hand-maintained ratings are subjective and may lag product changes; free tiers change frequently.
  • HN/Reddit mention counts are relative signals, not active-user counts, and carry brand noise for big names.
  • Most tracked apps are closed-source, so the GitHub column is mostly empty by design.
